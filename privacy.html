Haptic World Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Haptic World is an interactive visual and haptic experience app developed by Youssef Idris.

Information we collect

Haptic World does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone recordings, or user-generated content.

Motion and haptics

The app may read device motion sensors while a world is open to control visual effects. Motion data is processed on the device and is not transmitted to us. Haptic feedback is generated locally by the device.

Local settings

Preferences such as sound, haptics, motion control, visual quality, and particle density may be stored locally on your device.

Purchases

Optional world unlocks and tips are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Purchases are subject to the platform's terms and privacy policy.

Analytics and advertising

The current version does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version will be published at this URL.
